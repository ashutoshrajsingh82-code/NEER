# NEER Model Card

**Project:** Neural Embedding based Estimation and Reconstruction (SIH26066)
**Organization:** MoES / INCOIS
**Card scope:** bundled repository snapshot, audited 2026-09-29

## Status and intended use

NEER is an engineering and research prototype. The bundled checkpoint is usable for exercising the inference pipeline with the bundled synthetic dataset. It is not validated for operational ocean forecasting, scientific inference, safety-critical decisions, or public advisory use.

## Architecture

The model uses a convolutional encoder, a Vision Transformer representation, and a depth-conditioned decoder. The optional grid GNN and uncertainty head are disabled by default. The encoder produces a domain-pooled embedding and the decoder predicts one target value per configured depth. Current inference therefore does not produce a spatially resolved learned temperature field.

The preprocessing contract defines 11 input channels in `src/data/preprocessing/channels.py`: seven gridded surface fields (`sst`, `sss`, `sla`, `u_current`, `v_current`, `u_wind`, `v_wind`) plus cyclic time and normalized latitude/longitude features. Training targets are normalized absolute `subsurface_temp`, not temperature anomalies. The serving path applies training-set center/scale statistics to obtain temperature in physical units. Where a climatology is available, anomaly is calculated as `temperature - climatology`.

## Data and training evidence

- The checked-in demo dataset and tensor bundle are marked `DEMO_SYNTHETIC` / synthetic and are generated by `scripts/create_demo_data.py`.
- The demo tensor bundle has 24 monthly steps, 15 depths and a 101 × 241 grid. Targets are substantially masked; loss and evaluation use observation masks.
- Its chronological splits are 16 train, 4 validation, and 4 test timesteps. Normalization metadata records a training-only fit window.
- Checkpoint metadata records a training run and epoch history. This is evidence of software training on the synthetic bundle, not evidence of training on observational data.
- `data/raw/` contains no real ARGO profiles or gridded source dataset in this checkout.
- The shipped climatology asset is present, but its independent source and period provenance were not established in this audit. Treat climatology-dependent outputs as demonstration values until provenance is supplied and checked.

## Evaluation and validation

The repository implements masked metrics, baseline comparison, test-split evaluation, and an ARGO validation workflow. This audit exercised software paths and synthetic fixtures. It did not establish performance on an independent real-data holdout, observational ARGO matchup, calibrated uncertainty, or operational deployment. Demo ARGO reports use generated profiles and must not be described as independent observational validation.

Do not interpret any score or chart produced from `DEMO_SYNTHETIC` as evidence of scientific accuracy. A scientifically meaningful assessment requires traceable real data, documented quality control and matchup rules, independent temporally held-out observations, baseline comparisons, and domain-expert review.

## Known limitations

- The decoder output is domain pooled. Spatially varying anomaly maps result from subtracting local climatology from a domain-pooled temperature value; they are not pixel-wise learned temperature reconstructions.
- Synthetic data dominate the checked-in end-to-end path. Real source data were not available for this audit.
- Climatology provenance is incomplete in the checked-in asset.
- The default uncertainty output is disabled; where enabled, the code does not claim calibration.
- The frontend build was verified, but the complete dashboard was not manually tested across browsers and screen sizes in this audit.

## Recommended use

Use the repository for software development, API integration, reproducible demo workflows, and testing with clearly labeled synthetic data. Do not use the bundled model or synthetic evaluation to support oceanographic conclusions or operational decisions.
